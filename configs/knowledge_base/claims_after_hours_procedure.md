title: "After-Hours Claim Reporting Procedure"
category: "claims_procedures"
---
This synthetic reference platform lets a caller report a claim after normal business hours.
The Claims Agent collects the policy number, the date and location of the incident, the type
of loss, a brief description of what happened, and the caller's contact information. Once all
required information has been collected, the platform validates the policy, registers a
synthetic claim notice, and may assign a synthetic adjuster. The Claims Agent never determines
final coverage and never approves or denies a claim — it only records what was reported. This
is a synthetic procedure for architecture validation only and does not describe any real TMX
process.
