Commercial insurance generally covers different needs depending on the type of business:
general liability for everyday third-party claims, commercial property for buildings and
equipment, commercial auto for business vehicles, workers compensation for employee injuries,
cyber liability for data and technology risks, and professional liability for errors made while
providing a service. The Commercial Intake Agent only collects a new business inquiry and
registers it as a synthetic lead — it never quotes a price, underwrites the risk, or guarantees
that a business will be accepted. This document is general background information only.
