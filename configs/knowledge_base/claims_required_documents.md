title: "Documents Typically Requested After a Claim Is Reported"
category: "claims_procedures"
---
After a synthetic claim notice is registered on this reference platform, a synthetic adjuster
may later ask for supporting documents such as photographs of the damage, a police or incident
report if one was filed, and receipts for any items that need to be replaced. The Claims Agent
itself does not collect or store these documents — document upload is out of scope for this
reference platform. Providing documents promptly can help an adjuster review a claim, but does
not itself determine the outcome. This is synthetic guidance for architecture validation only.
