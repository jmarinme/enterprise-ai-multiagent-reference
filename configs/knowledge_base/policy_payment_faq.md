title: "What Policy Payment Status Means"
category: "policy_faq"
---
On this synthetic reference platform, a policy's payment status simply reflects whether the
most recent synthetic payment on record is current or overdue. A payment issue on a policy
does not automatically affect whether a claim can be reported or a commission can be reviewed
— it is surfaced as a fact, not used to block those flows. Only a Broker Services Agent or
Claims Agent Tool result can confirm a policy's real synthetic payment status; this document is
general background information only, never an authoritative source for a specific policy.
